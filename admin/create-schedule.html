Section Merging

Small sections can be split and merged with other classes of the same subject to reduce the number of classes. Partitions must have at least 5 students, with up to 3 partitions per subject, and are only created when they eliminate a class.

Choose when sections may split:

  • Small: Keep sections that fill at least half the room.
  • Two-thirds: Split a 25/40 section, but keep a 30/40 section.
  • Any: Split whenever it removes a class.

Automatic section merging must be enabled.

Lunch Break

Generate skips lunch by default. You can still schedule a class during lunch by editing its times, with a confirmation prompt. 12:00–1:00 remains greyed out for visibility.
