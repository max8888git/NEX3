Curriculums

Start from the colleges, then open the curriculums uploaded under one of them. Each curriculum is a single table covering every subject, grouped by year level and term with subtotals. Use Add curriculum here to upload a CSV without leaving this page.

Colleges

Open a college to see the curriculums filed under it. When a file’s hours per week do not match the units, the curriculum is still saved: the hours per week from the file are used, with a warning. If hours are missing, they are worked out as 1 lecture unit = 1 hour/week and 1 lab unit = 3 hours/week. Lecture and lab room types are chosen separately, and each prerequisite column holds one course id.
