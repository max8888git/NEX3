Create Sections

Open sections for one program and year level at a time. Students are split evenly with a 40-student cap; you can override a section’s headcount afterward.

How it works. There is one current academic year and semester at a time, for the whole campus. Every section you create — every college, every year level — belongs to that offering until the semester ends. Then archive the whole offering. Archived offerings are listed only by academic year and semester, not by college or class. Curriculum year (for example 2018-2019 vs 2024-2025) is only a version label and does not have to match the offering year.

Offering term

Schools do not run two academic years or semesters as the live offering at once. The term you start here applies to every college and year level until you archive it.

Archived Sections

Each row is one past academic year and semester — not per college. Open an offering to view its sections. Unarchive restores that entire term. Delete removes it permanently.
